# The Crusher King (World 1 boss)

**References looked at (Oct 3, 2026, Brave/Google Images):** It Takes Two's Toolbox boss (a worn real object at giant scale, its face made from its own parts, warm work light); stacked wooden ABC toy blocks (chipped paint, bare beech edges); voxel pixel-king dioramas (red velvet cape with ermine, big beard, tall gothic throne back with a crest, darker voxels low/back); painted wooden toy kings and nutcrackers (glossy paint, simple painted faces). Also checked retro 3D (PS1/N64, King Bob-omb): readable silhouettes, but the full style clashes with Pixel Plains' real-material look; the user picked the voxel king.

**Direction:** a pixel-art king built from real painted wooden toy cubes ("a giant pixel king made of stacked blocks", Story Line). Purple tunic with gold buttons, crimson velvet cape with an ermine collar, brown beard and mustache, gold crown with ruby/sapphire cubes, iron gauntlet fists with a tangerine cuff (Kai's freeze target, art-direction §6), Glitch-magenta static cubes on his right cheek and chest (the infection). Wrist/shoulder crystals stay teal neon (Miko's pound targets).

**Build:** `tools/crusher_king/kit.py` (voxel grid per grey-box part, buried cubes culled, 1-segment chamfer, every cube face UV'd over a whole swatch so the bevel lands on the worn rim). Atlas `textures/king_palette.png` from `textures/make_king_palette.py` (21 colors × 2 variants). Sizes = the grey-box parts in `Greybox` (BOSS ARENA). Source `blender_assets/CrusherKing.blend`, export `CrusherKing.fbx` (embed textures: a stripped texture path blocks the Studio import). Skins `King*` in `ArtService/PixelPlains.luau`.
